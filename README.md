# Peace for Palestine
## Two Nations, One Future

This repo contains the documents and software for the **Peace for Palestine** movement. It is It is a framework for finding peace in the Mideast and is the start of a 
negotiation between the world community, the Palestinian regions, and Israel.
Some of the ideas require policy changes in Palestine, Israel, and the work community. Some ideas are targeted to the citizens of Israel. Others require action from
the Palestinians. No movement can demand a change in leadership, but if people to buy into the process, then change is possible. It is important to note, that this 
document requires policy changes in Israel, but is external to Israel. It cannot be a demand, but part of a 3 way
conversation. Note that the document focuses on the people of Israel, Gaza, and the West Bank, but could also apply to Lebanon and Syria.
The document preserves the end goal of removing threats caused by extremist groups whether they be religious extreme Zionist,
or religious extreme terror groups, or JIhadists.
